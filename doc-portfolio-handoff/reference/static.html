Ideas are the beginning. Systems are the result.

We build software and connected systems around real business needs, from the first product decisions to the engineering that keeps everything working.

AdTech · Connected Systems

Adlify

Connecting mobile advertising screens with campaign intelligence.

Adlify is a digital-signage advertising platform for car-mounted screens. Screens pair securely and retrieve their assigned playlists, while a rule-based scheduler controls when and where ads play. GPS data feeds a live fleet map and heat maps, impressions are billed by zone, and advertisers monitor their campaigns through a dedicated portal.

Issue #04

Industrial AI · Computer Vision

Katakit

Replacing manual biscuit checks with real-time computer vision.

An automated quality-control system for the Digestive biscuit production line at Katakit, a Syrian food manufacturer. A camera above the conveyor feeds a real-time computer-vision pipeline that inspects biscuits in place of manual checks.

What it does

  • Watershed segmentation of touching biscuits
  • Detects cracks, size and shape issues, and burnt or undercooked biscuits
  • Live dashboard, alerts and exportable reports
  • Recommendations that point to potential root causes
  • Computer Vision
  • Image Segmentation
  • Quality Control
  • Dashboards
