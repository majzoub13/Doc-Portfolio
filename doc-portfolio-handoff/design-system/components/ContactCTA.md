# ContactCTA

The contact section block: a featured panel with halftone, eyebrow, `heading-1` title, one-sentence body, the EmailLink (with copy) on the left; a speech bubble, primary button and social links on the right.

## Props
`eyebrow`, `title`, `body`, `email`, `primary` (`{label, href}`), `bubble` (`{speaker, text}`, optional), `socials` (SocialLink props[]).

## Layout
7 / 5 split; one column under 700px with the email first. One per page.
