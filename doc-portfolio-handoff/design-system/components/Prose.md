# Prose

Body text. Inter 400, `body` (17px / 1.65, 16px on mobile), `text-primary`, max 68ch. `lead` switches to `body-lg` (20px / 1.6, 18px mobile) for the first paragraph under a hero or case-study header; `secondary` sets `text-secondary`.

## Rules
- Paragraph spacing 1em. No justified text, no uppercase paragraphs, no display face in paragraphs.
- Inline `code` uses `mono-code` on `bg-secondary` with a 1px `border-divider`.
- Keep prose on `bg-page`, `bg-panel` or `bg-secondary` — never directly over halftone or a photo.
