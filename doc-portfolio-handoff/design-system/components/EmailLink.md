# EmailLink

The email address as a headline: Barlow Condensed 700 at 28 → 48px, a yellow marker under the lower third that floods the whole word on hover. `copy` adds an IconButton that copies the address and announces "Copied" in a polite live region.

## Props
`address` (required), `copy` (boolean).

## Rules
Show the real address as text (not "Email me"). `overflow-wrap: anywhere` keeps long addresses inside 320px screens.
