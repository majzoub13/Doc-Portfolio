# SpeedLines

Horizontal motion lines of varied length and weight (1.5–4.5px), aligned to one edge.

## Props
`count` (default 9), `width`, `height`, `direction` (`right` — lines anchor to the right edge · `left`), `fluid` (fills its box).

## Limits
Behind a closing callout, beside a "shipped" moment, or leading into a section transition. Lines are `text-primary` at full strength on small sizes, `line-ink`/`line-paper` when they fill a spread. Never animate them continuously.
